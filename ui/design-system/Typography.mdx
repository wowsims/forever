# Typography

Tokens from `ui/styles/theme/typography.css`, faces from `ui/styles/base.css`. `1rem` is 14px below `fhd` (1921px) and 16px from `fhd` on (`--font-size-root` in `base.css`), so every `rem` below scales with it.

## Font faces

The woff2 files in `assets/fonts` against the `@font-face` rules `base.css` declares. A file with no rule is never loaded; a bold or italic weight without one is synthesised by the browser.

## Headings

`h1` to `h4` are fluid below the `xl` breakpoint and fixed from it; `h5` and `h6` are fixed. `text-fluid-xl` and `text-fluid-5xl` are the two fluid `@utility` sizes in `main.css`.
