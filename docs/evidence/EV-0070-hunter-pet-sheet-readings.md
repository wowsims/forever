# EV-0070: Tamed pet attack power, health and inherited stats from pet sheets

- Claim: a tamed pet's own attack power is the creature dps × 4.2 rounded down with nothing from Strength, its health is one reading that the creature health fits, and its armor, attack power and crit are not linear in the owner's.
- Sources: pet sheets read in game at levels 11, 17 and 30; nothing else recorded
- Game build: not recorded
- Date: not recorded
- Pull request: #509, whose body is empty; the readings are the text of the sites

## Attack power

The sheets of a level 11 Carrion Bird, a level 17 Cat and a level 30 pet read 36, 61 and 103. Each is the creature dps × 4.2 rounded down (30% of the creature dps as attack power, 14 a point of dps), with the owner's 10% rounded down beside it.

## Health

The one reading is 550 with Great Stamina 2 at 35 owner Stamina. The creature health fits it with about 1.1 health a point of the owner's Stamina, and half of it with the warlock's 7.5. The creature health is kept until a second reading at another Stamina.

## Inherited stats

The owner's expertise, the pet's armor, attack power and crit were read on a level 17 pet sheet at two gear states. They are not linear in the owner's stats.

## Ranged crit

A level 17 pet sheet read the owner's ranged crit whole at 84 of 85 bow skill, where the sim takes 0.04% off. A reading at a skill well below the maximum would decide.
