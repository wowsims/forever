# EV-0057: Holy Light and Flash of Light make 0.25 threat a point healed

- Claim: Holy Light and Flash of Light make 0.25 threat a point healed, half the rate of other heals, and Iron Creed's
  damage reduction comes from making a Holy Strike whether or not it lands.
- Sources: in game threat testing; no counts recorded
- Game build: not recorded
- Date: not recorded
- Pull request: #636

The testing also confirmed rules the sim already followed: overhealing makes no threat, heal threat is split between the
enemies in combat, Seal of Light and Judgement of Light heals make 0.5 threat a point healed, Righteous Fury (x1.9 with
the talent) and Salvation (x0.7) reach all four heals, mana potions make 0.5 threat a point of mana restored split
between enemies, and Touch of the Grave's damage makes normal threat while its heal makes none. Lay on Hands and Holy
Shock were not tested.
