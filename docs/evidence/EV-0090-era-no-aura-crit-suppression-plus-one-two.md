# EV-0090: No aura crit suppression against targets 1 or 2 levels above, from Classic Era logs

- Claim: the extra crit suppression from aura crit appears against targets 3 or more levels above only; targets 1 or 2 levels above add none.
- Sources: Classic Era logs, not Forever: 818 Blackwing Lair and Ahn'Qiraj 40 reports on Warcraft Logs, hunter Auto Shot (it cannot glance, so it never meets the crit cap). Each comparison stays within one player, pull block and set of crit buffs, with mob levels that three independent community sources agree on
- Game build: not recorded
- Date: not recorded
- Pull request: #202

| ---------------------- | ----------- | -------------------- | ------------ |

| Level 62 to 63 targets | 2.20 ± 0.43 | 2.8                  | 1.6          |
| Level 60 to 63 targets | 4.26 ± 0.35 | 4.8                  | 4.8          |

The extra part appears at +3 only. Its size here (about 1.3%) is below the 1.8% a community test page measured, and the two raids differ by about 2 sigma, so the 1.8% value stays.
