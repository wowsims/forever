# EV-0080: Demon mana regen is a share of the warlock's Intellect at level 30

- Claim: a demon's mana regen is its share of the warlock's Intellect as MP5, 30% for the Imp and 10% for the Voidwalker, Sayaad and Felhunter, with nothing from the demon's own stats.
- Sources: in-game pet panes and tooltips of a level 30 warlock with 110 Intellect; nothing else recorded
- Game build: not recorded
- Date: not recorded

The pet panes read Mana Regen 33 for the Imp and 11 for the Voidwalker and Sayaad. The tooltips say "Increased by 30% / 10% of Master's Intellect". That is exactly the share times the warlock's Intellect.

The regen shares are read at level 30 only. The Felhunter's 10% follows the other demons: it could not be learned on the beta, so no Felhunter pane was read. The pane does not show the demon's own Intellect, so the pane totals cannot split out the warlock's share of the demon's maximum mana.
